A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Connecting people, information, and ideas.

My background brings together design, communication, education, and information services. I earned a BFA in Integrated Design from Parsons School of Design and am currently pursuing a Master of Library and Information Science at San José State University.

Through my education and professional experiences, I've developed an interest in how people discover, access, and engage with information. I'm particularly drawn to projects that combine research, visual communication, technology, and user-centered thinking.

Whether developing digital resources, designing communication materials, or helping others navigate information, I value making complex ideas approachable and creating experiences that are both functional and engaging.
